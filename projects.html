Merchant inventory optimizer

Gordón for DoorDash

Scan a barcode and Gordón estimates shelf life and tracks live usage. As items near expiry it promotes the dishes that use them so they sell through instead of getting thrown out. When stock runs low it keeps the item live for a limited number of orders, then switches it off. No spoilage, no stockouts.
