Handoff

Offboarding agent

An autonomous offboarding agent for the moment an engineer resigns. Handoff maps everything they own, finds what would “die with them,” interviews them about the reasoning that never made it into a doc, and then runs the handover itself — reassigning Jira tickets, booking transfer sessions, and notifying the team in Slack. FalkorDB holds the ownership knowledge graph, LaserData streams every event, RocketRide orchestrates analyze → interview → plan → execute, and Guild.ai acts in Jira, email, Slack and calendars.

Gordón — DoorDash Merchant Inventory

Inventory optimizer for DoorDash merchants

Scan a barcode and Gordón estimates shelf life and tracks live usage. As items near expiry it suggests promotions on the related dishes so they sell through instead of getting thrown out, and when stock runs low it keeps an item live for a limited number of orders and then auto-disables it — no spoilage, no stockouts. Nexla feeds operational context through an MCP, Zero.xyz supplies barcode, weather and food-safety lookups, Pomerium gates Staff / Chef / Manager / Owner roles, and OpenAI is the reasoning layer.
