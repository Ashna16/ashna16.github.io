Gordón for DoorDash

GORDÓN is an inventory optimizer for DoorDash merchants. Once the restaurant scans a barcode, the system estimates shelf life and tracks live usage of that inventory. As items get closer to expiry, GORDÓN suggests promotions on the related dishes so they sell through faster instead of getting thrown out.

DropPoint for Spotify

We all have that one moment in a song that hits differently. Spotify tracks every song you play, but it has no idea that moment exists. So here's my idea: DropPoint. Pin any moment in a song. Tag how it makes you feel. And let Spotify generate playlists based on that feeling, in that moment.
