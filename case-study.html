DOORDASH_Merchant Inventory

GORDÓN is an inventory optimizer for DoorDash merchants. Once the restaurant scans a barcode, the system estimates shelf life and tracks live usage of that inventory. As items get closer to expiry, GORDÓN suggests promotions on the related dishes so they sell through faster instead of getting thrown out. If inventory is too low to keep fulfilling DoorDash orders, it can also keep an item live for a limited number of orders and then auto-disable it , so the store avoids stockout and bad customer experiences.

Youtube_Learning Sandbox

YouTube is one of the best places to learn to code — but the format hasn't changed in over a decade. You open a tutorial, set up your editor, hit an error, switch tabs to fix it, come back, lose your place... and somewhere in all that, the actual learning gets lost. So I asked: what if YouTube had an Interactive Sandbox Mode? Pause the tutorial, open a live sandbox, try the code yourself — right there. Run it, get hints, no tab-switching, no separate editor. I built a working MVP to test it out.

Google Maps x Event Discovery

We always know where we're going and how long we'll be there — but what's happening around us during that time? That's still something we have to go searching for separately, in a different app, at a different moment. There's a simpler way. You open Google Maps, enter your destination, and add how long your task will take. Maps surfaces events happening nearby before you arrive and after you're done — concerts, meetups, food festivals, pulled live from Luma, Eventbrite, and Google Events.

Spotify_Pin Your Moment

We all have that one moment in a song that hits differently. That exact part in Blinding Lights where the synth kicks in and you feel unstoppable. Spotify tracks every song you play. But it has no idea that moment exists. So here's my idea — DropPoint. Pin any moment in a song. Tag how it makes you feel. Energized. Nostalgic. Melancholy. And instead of generating playlists based on your favourite artists — Spotify generates them based on that feeling, in that moment.
