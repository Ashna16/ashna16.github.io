DOORDASH_Merchant Inventory

GORDÓN is an inventory optimizer for DoorDash merchants. Once the restaurant scans a barcode, the system estimates shelf life and tracks live usage of that inventory. As items get closer to expiry, GORDÓN suggests promotions on the related dishes so they sell through faster instead of getting thrown out. If inventory is too low to keep fulfilling DoorDash orders, it can also keep an item live for a limited number of orders and then auto-disable it , so the store avoids stockout and bad customer experiences.

We used:

Nexla — I created an MCP connected through Nexla that helps us fetch data from our database and return it to our agents, so operational context (orders, waste, preferences, inventory) flows into the decision loop in real time.

Zero.xyz — paid x402 lookups for barcode identity, live weather, and food-safety signals that feed demand and kitchen restrictions into those decisions.

Pomerium — identity-aware role gating so Staff / Chef / Manager / Owner permissions control who can approve promotions and change availability.

OpenAI — the reasoning layer behind GORDÓN, turning inventory, demand, and risk signals into clear recommendations and kitchen-ready promotion copy managers can act on.

Cursor — the development environment I used to build, iterate, and ship the full stack
