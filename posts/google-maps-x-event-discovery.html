If I were a PM · June 2026

Google Maps Event Discovery

What's happening nearby while you're there

Open on YouTubeLinkedIn post

We always know where we're going and how long we'll be there. What's happening around us during that time is still something we have to go searching for separately, in a different app, at a different moment.

The idea

You open Google Maps, enter your destination, and add how long your task will take. Maps surfaces events happening nearby before you arrive and after you're done: concerts, meetups, food festivals, pulled live from Luma, Eventbrite and Google Events.

Google Maps already owns the “where” and the “when.” This feature owns the “what else.”
