Google Maps x Event Discovery

We always know where we're going and how long we'll be there — but what's happening around us during that time? That's still something we have to go searching for separately, in a different app, at a different moment.

There's a simpler way.

You open Google Maps, enter your destination, and add how long your task will take. Maps surfaces events happening nearby before you arrive and after you're done — concerts, meetups, food festivals, pulled live from Luma, Eventbrite, and Google Events.

Google Maps already owns the "where" and the "when." This feature owns the "what else."
