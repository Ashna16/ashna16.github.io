Live Coach for Strava

A coach in your ear mid-run, not a report after it

Open on YouTubeCode on GitHub

Strava is the gold standard for tracking. But while you're hitting the wall at mile four, it stays silent. It only tells you what went wrong after the run is over.

The idea

Live Coach turns that data into a live conversation: a real-time voice agent that fills the mid-run feedback gap. Whether it's a mid-stride pace correction or a dynamic reroute when you're red-lining, it's like having a professional coach in your ear for every mile.

How it was built

  • Voice: Vapi for seamless, low-latency coaching conversations
  • State: Redis to store and react to pace data and session trends instantly
  • Context: Senso to anchor the agent in the runner's training history and long-term goals
