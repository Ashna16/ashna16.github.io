Platforms I led in this role

01

TMS: Transaction Management System

New credit risk monitoring platform

  • Owned the product roadmap for a platform that monitors 1,000 to 2,000 corporate borrowers (US$15B+ in loans) every quarter-end. Review time fell from 4 days to about 1 day for 5 global teams.
  • Led business, engineering and regional stakeholders through a parallel migration for hundreds of users, validating new-vs-old cohorts before cutover. Adoption passed 90%.
  • Designed the analyst review experience: one consolidated view of parent and subsidiary financials with prior-quarter context.
  • Owned data integrity across releases, validating persistence and reconciliation in SQL ahead of go-live.

Analysts enter financial metrics, borrower reviews and loan covenant details each quarter, and the platform tracks compliance, trends and lending risk. Recognized in RBC’s Major Projects success deck and in Quant & Tech Services’ Recognized Contributions 2024.
